FOR DIYUU, WITH LOVE ♡

A responsive romantic mini-website made with the photos and videos you uploaded.

HOW TO OPEN
1. Extract For_Diyuu_Love_Website.zip.
2. Open the folder.
3. Open index.html in a browser (Chrome recommended).
   On Android, you can keep the folder and open index.html from a local web server/editor such as Spck Editor.

FILES
- index.html: page content
- style.css: design, responsive layout and animations
- script.js: scrolling effects, compliments and floating hearts
- assets/: your 3 photos and 3 videos

NOTES
- Internet is only needed to load the optional Google Fonts. The website itself uses local photos and videos and does not need an API, account, or paid service.
- If a video doesn't play in a particular preview app, open index.html in Chrome.
- You can replace the text or media by editing index.html and keeping the asset filenames unchanged.
